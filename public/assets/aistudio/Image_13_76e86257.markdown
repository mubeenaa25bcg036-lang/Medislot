## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an environment of clinical precision married to approachable SaaS simplicity. Designed for an AI-powered healthcare appointment platform, the interface must inspire immediate trust, alleviate patient anxiety, and reduce cognitive load during high-intent scheduling flows.

The design philosophy combines **Clean Healthcare Minimalism** with high-utility **Modern SaaS ergonomics**. It features pristine white canvases, soft architectural surfaces, crystalline medical blues, and restorative teal accents. Typography is structured and unobtrusive, while micro-surfaces rely on subtle pill geometry, gentle layered elevation, and hairline borders to convey institutional competence without bureaucratic friction.

## Colors

The color system delivers high legibility and reassurance through a strict light-mode palette:

- **Primary (`#0284C7`) & Sky Accent (`#0EA5E9`)**: The core medical blue anchor. Conveys clinical reliability, technical intelligence, and calm authority. Used for primary calls-to-action, key selected states, active dates, and AI diagnostic highlights.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488`) & Teal Accent (`#14B8A6`)**: Restorative botanical teal. Communicates wellness, positive outcomes, and automated triage confirmations. Applied to confirmation chips, available time-slot tags, and success notifications.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces**:
  - `Base Canvas`: Pure clinical white (`#FFFFFF`).
  - `Subtle Surface`: Ice slate (`#F8FAFC`) for secondary panels, schedule tracks, and disabled states.
  - `Hover & Structural Neutral`: Cool slate tint (`#F1F5F9`) for interactive cell backgrounds and segmented controls.
  - `Borders & Separators`: Hairline slate (`#E2E8F0`) to provide crisp, quiet boundaries.
- **Text & Content**:
  - Primary text: Deep slate (`#0F172A`) for high-contrast, effortless legibility.
  - Secondary / Supporting: Mid-slate (`#64748B`) for metadata, sub-labels, and doctor credentials.
  - Muted / Placeholder: Neutral slate (`#94A3B8`).

## Typography

Typography relies exclusively on `Inter` to project neutrality, clarity, and precision. 

Headlines utilize slight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) and medium/semibold weights to anchor complex forms and doctor profiles. Body text maintains open line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to ensure high readability of medical histories, appointment instructions, and triage summaries. System labels and metadata utilize uppercase tracking or medium-weight micro-labels for rapid scanning across calendars and time slots.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt modular scale deployed across a 12-column responsive fluid grid system:

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer container padding (max container width: 1200px for booking flows, 1440px for provider portals).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins. Multi-column appointment schedulers collapse from horizontal day-views to vertical list flows.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` margins and gutters. Calendars switch to horizontal scroll carousels for date selection and single-column stacked physician cards.
- **Density**: Booking widgets and clinic calendars preserve generous internal padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`) to prevent mis-taps on medical triage selections and doctor slot buttons.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean atmospheric depth rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing clinical sterility and digital agility:

- **Hairline Rest (`Flat`)**: All default cards, summary modules, and inactive calendar days sit directly on `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`, enclosed by a 1px border in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Low Elevation (`shadow-sm`)**: Used for persistent booking sidebars, floating doctor chips, and search input states: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Medium Elevation (`shadow-md`)**: Used for selected appointment cards, floating action drawers, and modal sheets: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Layering**: Modals and AI chat drawers deploy subtle backdrop blurs (`backdrop-blur-md` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`) backed by a faint blue-tinted overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.2)`).

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a dual shape hierarchy: structured containers pair with pill-shaped interactive micro-elements.

- **Primary Cards & Modals**: Utilize `rounded-xl` (1rem / 16px) for inner booking cards and `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for prominent dashboard containers, AI summary cards, and calendar viewports.
- **Controls & Secondary Elements**: Standard form inputs, dropdowns, and button sets use base `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px to 0.75rem / 12px).
- **Pill Geometry**: Time slots, status chips, doctor specialty tags, and AI prompt bubbles adopt full pill rounding (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to soften technical interactions and signal distinct tap targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid medical blue (`#0284C7`), text white, `rounded-lg` or `rounded-full` for CTA paths. On hover: `#0369A1`. Focus: `0 0 0 3px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pure white background with `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text. On hover: `#F8FAFC` background with `#0284C7` border and text.
- **Teal Confirmation**: Solid `#0D9488` with white text, reserved for final "Confirm Appointment" state. Hover: `#0F766E`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical padding, `space-sm` horizontal padding.
  - *Confirmed / Available*: Background `#CCFBF1`, text `#0F766E`.
  - *In-Review / AI Suggestion*: Background `#E0F2FE`, text `#0369A1`.
  - *Unavailable / Past*: Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#64748B`.
- **Doctor Specialty Chips**: Background `#F8FAFC`, border `#E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`, font size `label-md`.

### Cards & Appointment Blocks
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border `#E2E8F0`, rounded with `rounded-xl` or `rounded-2xl`.
- Padding: `space-lg` (24px).
- Interactive Doctor Card: Light hover elevation (`shadow-md`), subtle border color transition to `#0EA5E9`. Includes physician avatar with online status indicator, verified badge, rating tag, and available time slot pill rack.

### Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `#E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- Height: 44px (touch friendly). Focus border: `#0284C7` with a 3px ring in `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.12)`.
- AI Natural Language Input: Extended height input with trailing teal spark icon (`#0D9488`), subtle glass effect (`bg-white/90`), and rounded-2xl geometry.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 18px x 18px. Border: 1.5px solid `#CBD5E1`.
- Checked State: Solid `#0284C7` with white checkmark icon or radio dot. Focus ring: `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.2)`.

### Time-Slot Grid Matrix
- Discrete buttons (`h-10`, `px-4`, `rounded-full`, text `label-md`).
- Default: Border `#E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`.
- Selected: Background `#0284C7`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `#0284C7`, `shadow-sm`.
- AI Recommended Slot: Border 1.5px solid `#0D9488`, faint teal background tint (`#F0FDFA`), with a micro badge denoting "Fastest Care".
